# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Three.js for the rendered gallery, as requested. Implementation uses the Sites Vinext starter and hosted object storage.

## Product Purpose

Turn a set of 1–50 photographs into a first-person exhibition that guests can explore from a shared link.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The creator supplies the initial photographs. Their count determines the number of rooms and placement of works.
- The visitor can enter without an account. The creator controls publication and can copy a separate exhibition link.
- Preserve photo aspect ratios. Make an individual photograph easy to inspect at a larger size.

## Operating Context

One browser flow for preparing and publishing an exhibition, followed by a first-person walk on desktop or mobile. A photo index provides direct access to the works.

## Evidence on Hand

The user has not supplied photographs yet. The demonstration exhibition is clearly labeled as a sample.
